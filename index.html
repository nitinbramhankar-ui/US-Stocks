Holdings & maximum entry prices

Maximum entry price targets a 20% CAGR over 3 years
Model: five consecutive quarters; QoQ growth weighted 10% / 20% / 30% / 40% from oldest to newest; annualised as (1 + weighted QoQ growth)^4 − 1 and capped at 200%. Optimistic growth decays by 25% annually; conservative growth decays by 50% annually. Year 3 entry price = Year 3 fair value ÷ 1.728. Current diluted shares are held constant. Amazon requires segment-level AWS and retail inputs for the agreed SOTP model; the current Worker revenue endpoint returns consolidated SEC revenue only, so AMZN valuation is not recalculated automatically here.
Data coverage: Cloudflare Worker currently exposes quote and SEC revenue endpoints for the symbols configured in its code. Non-US issuers, private companies, and unsupported symbols may show unavailable financial data. Missing financials are never invented. Your historical valuation values are retained until a valid recalculation is possible.
