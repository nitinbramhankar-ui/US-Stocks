Select a stock and enter its latest five consecutive quarterly revenues (oldest to newest), contribution margin, base EV multiple, net cash/(debt), and diluted shares. The model applies 10%/20%/30%/40% chronological weights to QoQ growth, annualises quarterly growth, caps starting growth at 200%, and applies the agreed optimistic/conservative decay curves. This refreshes the model from the inputs you provide; it does not independently fetch quarterly financial statements.

Holdings & entry prices

Maximum entry price is the primary decision metric
How to read this: “Max entry” is the maximum price today that meets the 20% CAGR hurdle over three years. “Below entry” means the live price is at or below that model threshold. Optimistic and conservative cases use the agreed growth-decay assumptions. Year 3 fair value is shown as secondary context.

Coverage note: QQQ and BITO are excluded. SPCX is included in the model, but its symbol may not be available from Finnhub; if so, its quote will show as unavailable. This page refreshes market quotes; it does not automatically refresh quarterly financials, margins, valuation multiples, net cash, or the model assumptions. Those inputs remain the latest model snapshot until updated.
